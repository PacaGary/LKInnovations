Design Comparison — Option A

Three.js Particle Swarm

20,000 instanced particles arranged on a Fibonacci sphere with layered sine curl turbulence. Iridescent cyan-to-magenta hue drift, Fresnel shell shading, and Unreal Bloom post-processing. Feels alive — a breathing, swirling orb of light.

Best suited for a hero that reads as dynamic, scientific, and alive. The density and bloom create a sense of depth that rewards a full-screen hero placement.

Aesthetic

Iridescent particle field / sci-fi bioluminescence

Performance

InstancedMesh — single draw call regardless of count

Interactivity

Passive — camera orbits autonomously

Dependency

Three.js + EffectComposer (CDN, ~280 KB gz)
