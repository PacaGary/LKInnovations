Liquid Glass — Ray-Marched SDF

A single ray-marched signed distance field rendered in a full-screen WebGL fragment shader. IOR 1.5 refraction, chromatic aberration, Fresnel reflection off a procedural softbox environment. Drag to rotate — tilt tracks your pointer.

Best suited for a hero that reads as premium, tactile, and refined. The glass aesthetic connects to product and material — appropriate for a biotech consumer health brand.
