Transforming everyday allergen management

Pacagen is a consumer brand that engineers specialized proteins that neutralize various allergens in your home, formulated into products that can sit on your shelf, in your cabinets, or in your bathroom.

Unlike other treatments for pet and dust allergies, Pacagen's proactive solutions can safely protect you from allergy flare-ups before they occur.
